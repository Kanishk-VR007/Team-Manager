# Video Generation Prompt & Animation Guidelines

**Target File Name:** `coding-hackers.mp4`
**Resolution:** 1080p or 4K (16:9 Aspect Ratio)
**Format:** `.mp4`
**Behavior:** Seamless loop (if possible)

Use the following detailed prompt and instructions with your preferred AI video generator (e.g., Runway Gen-2, Pika Labs, Sora, or Midjourney + After Effects) to create the video background for the Register page.

## 1. Base Environment & Aesthetic
- **Setting:** A futuristic, ultra-realistic software mission control room. 
- **Lighting:** Cinematic lighting, dark mode enterprise UI aesthetics, cyberpunk undertones. 
- **Foreground/Midground:** Sleek desks with glowing blue holographic displays and glowing keyboards.
- **Background:** A panoramic, floor-to-ceiling glass window showing a modern tech campus at night.

## 2. Subject Animation (The Employees)
- **Action:** Several employees/hackers seated at the desks are actively working. 
- **Movement:** Their hands should be visibly moving and typing rapidly over the glowing keyboards. 
- **Behavior:** Include subtle realistic movements like leaning in to look at the screens, thinking (e.g., resting a hand on their chin), or pointing at the holographic displays.

## 3. Screen Animations (The Code)
- **Displays:** The holographic blue screens in front of the employees should not be static. 
- **Action:** Streams of code, telemetry data, and terminal logs should be dynamically typing out, scrolling upwards, or rapidly updating in real-time to match the typing speed of the employees.

## 4. Environmental Animations (The Sky & City)
- **Clouds:** Looking out through the panoramic glass windows into the night sky, there should be subtle, slow-drifting night clouds moving steadily across the horizon.
- **Northern Lights (Aurora):** Above the city skyline, incorporate shimmering, waving Northern Lights (aurora borealis) in hues of cyan, emerald, and deep blue, gently waving across the sky.
- **Cityscape:** Slight twinkling lights from the distant tech campus buildings to give life to the city.

## Final Output Instructions
Once the video is generated, ensure it is saved exactly as `coding-hackers.mp4` and placed directly inside the `d:\TeamManger\frontend\public\` directory. The React app is already configured to automatically load and loop this video behind your UI.
